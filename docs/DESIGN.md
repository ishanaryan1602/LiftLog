# Design system

FORM uses a deep plum/black default palette with white editorial type, violet primary actions, and semantic green/amber/red status tokens. A light mode is available from the compact switch in Settings. Accent choices live in Settings and are persisted locally. Purple is reserved for navigation, actions, and animated link underlines; amber remains a secondary status accent. The global navigation is a translucent, rounded dynamic-island pill anchored to the top-right, with a compact brand mark on the left and horizontal overflow on small screens. The layout intentionally uses negative space, oversized headings, and layered violet glows on the primary Push A card. Cards use thin borders, generous radii, and low-contrast surfaces; primary grids are intentionally uniform, while spacing and content hierarchy provide variation. Empty histories are omitted rather than wrapped in placeholder cards. Motion includes page entrance, staggered card reveals, hover lift, border glow, and animated active-link underlines, all respecting reduced-motion preferences.

Motion is limited to 200–400ms entrance/interaction transitions and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Mobile layouts keep workout actions thumb-friendly and switch grids to one column. Empty states explain what data is missing without fabricating progress. Shared Button, Input, Textarea, Card, Badge, and Calendar primitives provide shadcn-style customization instead of relying on browser-default controls. Measurements use the reusable month calendar and open their entry form in a focused date modal. Inputs have visible labels and focus states; errors should be concise and actionable.
